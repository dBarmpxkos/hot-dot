Principle of Operation

Hot-dot is a thermal haptic interface that converts content from a smartphone screen to thermal feedback. It uses the smartphone CPU to analyse the content under a user's finger and converts the region of interest to thermal signals, which are sent to an MCU that drives the microheaters.

  • Wearable finger-band: A Kapton-based ring with an array of SMT thermistors working as heaters, optional photodiodes, RP2040, peripheral electronics and USB-C.
  • Android script: Reads screen content around the touched area, translates it to Braille or hybrid texture modes, and communicates with the finger-band.
